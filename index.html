Chapter I

The Beverage

Pray, choose the cornerstone of your composition. Each is prepared by hand at the proprietor's bench.

Chapter II

The Temperament

At what climate shall thy cup be presented? The proprietor recommends 73°C, but indulges every preference.

Chapter III

The Bean

From cellared estates the world over. Each parcel is roasted in copper, in small batch, by Monsieur Devereaux.

Chapter IV

The Water

Drawn from quiet corners of the world — and one or two corners of myth — decanted at the precise temperament prescribed.

Chapter V

The Milk

Frothed soft as a cloud — or omitted, should the gentleman or lady prefer. The proprietor passes no judgement.

Chapter VI

The Embellishment

A measure of sweetness, perhaps a flourish of syrup. Discretion is the proprietor's hallmark.
